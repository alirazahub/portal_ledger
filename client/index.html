HOW IT WORKS

Track supplier invoices from submission to payment.

Record each invoice, save customer portal details, update the status, and keep the next step visible. Your team submits invoices through customer portals; Portal Ledger tracks the work around that process.

1. Record the invoice

Add the invoice number, customer, amount, legal entity, portal, dates, and responsible teammate.

2. Open the right portal

Keep portal URLs, payment terms, and submission requirements in one directory.

3. Track and follow up

Record submitted, needs info, approved, processing, rejected, or paid and keep follow-ups visible.

4. Close the loop

Attach supporting documents and record payment status so the team can find the invoice history later.

FAQ

What Portal Ledger does—and doesn’t do.

Does it submit invoices to customer portals?

No. Your team submits invoices in each customer’s portal, then records the status and reference here.

Does it replace accounting software or process payments?

No. It tracks invoice operations and follow-ups; it does not move money, file taxes, or replace accounting software.

Can I open attached documents?

Yes. Open supported PDF, PNG, and JPEG files from their document records.

Can all users change the currency?

No. The workspace owner controls currency settings; existing invoices keep their recorded currency.
